Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers. Verified Facts.

Is Snap Flow Studio free?
Yes. Snap Flow Studio is completely free. There are no paywalls, hidden watermark locks, or subscription requirements.
Where are screenshots stored and processed?
Screenshots are processed entirely in memory and cached in local browser IndexedDB storage on your computer. They are never sent to any remote server or third-party cloud.
Does Snap Flow Studio track my browsing history?
No. Snap Flow Studio operates under the activeTab permission model. It only inspects the active browser tab when you click a capture action, and never monitors other tabs or history.
Why can internal browser pages not be captured?
Google Chrome enforces strict security restrictions blocking all extensions from running content scripts on internal pages (chrome://, edge://, about:) and the Chrome Web Store gallery.
Which export formats are supported?
You can copy directly to your system clipboard as PNG, download Lossless PNG, compressed JPEG, modern WebP, or generate multi-page Smart-Split PDF files.
How does Smart-Split PDF avoid cutting text lines?
Before dividing tall images across pages, the exporter analyzes horizontal pixel gutters to detect whitespace between paragraphs, placing page breaks naturally in empty gutters.
