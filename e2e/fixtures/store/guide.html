Sample walking guide

Five stages around the harbour, one long weekend

A 42 km coastal loop with cliff paths, a tidal causeway and a lighthouse you can climb. This guide covers every stage from start to finish.

The stages

Each stage ends at a village with somewhere to eat and a bus back to the start, so the loop can be walked in one go or one piece at a time.

Stage 1: Old Quay to Amber Sands

7.5 km · 2 hours · Easy

The loop begins on the stone quay and follows the sea wall east. The surface is level and paved for the first three kilometres, then turns to firm sand above the high-water line. Amber Sands has a cafe open all year.

Stage 2: Amber Sands to Fernhead

9 km · 3 hours · Moderate

A steady climb through coastal woodland brings you onto the first cliff path. Waymarks are green discs on short posts. Allow extra time after rain, when the middle section is muddy and the wooden steps are slippery.

Stage 3: Fernhead to Causeway Point

8 km · 2.5 hours · Moderate

The tidal causeway can only be crossed for about three hours either side of low water. Check the tide table below before setting out. If the tide is wrong, the inland path adds 2 km and rejoins the route at the far end.

Stage 4: Causeway Point to Lantern Rock

10.5 km · 3.5 hours · Challenging

The longest stage and the one with the most climbing. The reward is the lighthouse at Lantern Rock, where 112 steps lead to a gallery with a view of the whole loop. The last bus leaves the car park at half past five.

Stage 5: Lantern Rock to Old Quay

7 km · 2 hours · Easy

A gentle descent through heathland and back along the harbour edge. Seals are often seen from the last headland in the early morning. The route ends where it began, beside the harbour office.

Before you go

Three things that make the walk easier.

Carry water

There are no taps between Fernhead and Lantern Rock, a stretch of more than 18 km.

Wear proper boots

Stages 2 and 4 cross rough ground. Trainers are fine for stages 1 and 5 only.

Tell someone

Mobile signal is patchy on the cliffs. Leave your route and return time with a friend.
